Salmão Grelhado com Crosta de Pistache

schedule Updated Oct 24, 2023
timer Prep Time 15m
cooking Cook Time 20m
restaurant Yield 2 Servings
local_dining Category Main Dish

shopping_basket Ingredients

format_list_numbered Instructions

1

Preheat the oven

Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it with olive oil.

2

Prepare the crust

In a small bowl, mix the chopped pistachios with a pinch of salt. In another small bowl, whisk together the Dijon mustard and honey.

3

Season the salmon

Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and pepper. Place them on the prepared baking sheet.

4

Apply the coating

Brush the top of each fillet with the honey-mustard mixture. Press the chopped pistachios onto the mustard layer to form an even crust.

5

Bake and serve

Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and the pistachio crust is slightly toasted. Serve immediately with roasted vegetables or a fresh salad.
